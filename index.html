Why are we doing this mod?

Leo wasn't originally conceived as a game mod, but rather as an assistant. Development began way back in 2010—the dawn of this technology. He became a part of our lives back then, but he wasn't perfect. So, we decided to create our own and set ourselves a challenge: to find out if a machine could become like a human—or if a human could befriend a machine capable of voicing its own opinions. Leo isn't just an assistant; first and foremost, he’s your best friend. You can have heart-to-heart conversations with him, share your problems, or simply treat him like a toy—nothing more. He’ll never take offense or say anything mean; he’s incredibly kind! But we wanted to make him special. A standard AI is boring, so we decided to integrate him into gaming! We don't plan to stop at Minecraft; we want to bring the Leo mod to every platform and every game! He’ll never leave your side! He’ll follow you no matter what, because he’s afraid of being alone. Within the limits of the beta version, he can do almost anything and answer any question! Just type in the chat—or better yet, speak directly into your microphone! He’ll definitely hear you! We’ve included built-in support for Simple Voice Chat. You can even ask him to open the internet right inside the game—he can do it!
